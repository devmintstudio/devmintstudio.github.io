MYRA — DEVMINT STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains how the Myra desktop application ("Myra"), developed by DevMint Studio, handles information. Myra is a Windows desktop application that runs on your own computer.

1. Information stored on your device

Myra stores its settings, your profile preferences, your API keys, chat history and memory on your own computer (in the application's data folder). This information stays on your device and is not uploaded to DevMint Studio servers.

2. Google sign-in and Google Workspace

If you choose to sign in with Google, Myra uses Google's OAuth sign-in to access the Google services you ask it to use (such as Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive and Docs) so it can carry out your voice commands, for example reading your calendar or helping with email. Myra only accesses this data when you are signed in and only to perform actions you request. Your Google sign-in tokens are stored locally on your device. DevMint Studio does not receive, store or sell your Google account data.

3. Your API keys and AI services

Myra works with AI service keys that you provide yourself (for example Google Gemini and LiveKit). Your conversations and commands are sent to those AI providers under your own keys so the assistant can respond. Their handling of that data is governed by those providers' own privacy policies.

4. License verification

To activate Myra and to provide the free trial, the application sends your device's unique device ID to our licensing service (hosted on Supabase) to check whether your device is licensed. No conversation content, files or personal documents are sent for license verification.

5. Voice

Voice interaction is processed through the AI/voice services connected with your keys. DevMint Studio does not record or store your voice on its own servers.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with the service providers you connect through your own keys and sign-ins, as described above.

7. Your control

You can sign out of Google inside Myra at any time, and you can remove Myra's data by deleting the application data folder on your computer. Uninstalling Myra does not automatically delete the data folder, so you stay in control of it.
